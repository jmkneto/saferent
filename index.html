How it works

Fixed rules decide the score, so every point can be explained.

  1. 01

    Enter the ad

    From the address and Eircode we find the area, then compare the price with the official RTB average rent for that size.

  2. 02

    Tick what happened

    Each warning sign adds fixed points. A price 40% or more under the average adds 40 on its own.

  3. 03

    Get a clear answer

    A risk level with next steps, and the Eircode ready to search on the RTB register.
